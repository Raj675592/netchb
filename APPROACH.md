# Approach and sample shipment notes

I started with NetCHB's published XML service information. Its public service directory lists `uploadEntry`, `amendEntry`, and status operations. The upload schema defines the `entry` root and the required `entry-no`, `header`, `manifest`, and `invoices` sections. I used the system-generated entry number form and left out `<transmit/>`; NetCHB documents that upload acceptance is not the same as CBP acceptance. I kept the XSD and NetCHB example request beside the code so the output structure is inspectable. A current Strix integration reference exposes files it describes as sourced from the live service schema.

For this shipment I read the invoice and bill of lading text and visually reviewed the scanned packing list and bill of lading. The invoice identifies invoice `KBAS/NB/26-0912`, buyer Northbound Outfitters, consignee Cascade Fulfillment Partners, five apparel lines, and six-digit HS headings. The B/L gives house bill `BMLVHCM26090418`, master bill `OPLUSNG260917735`, carrier `OPLU`, vessel `PACIFIC ARGOS`, voyage `041E`, ETA 14 October 2026, 138 cartons, and Tacoma as port of discharge. Tacoma is CBP port 3002. The packing list reports 1,888 kg gross and 13.58 CBM; the B/L reports 1,930 kg gross.

I preserved source line values ($22,899 combined) in the XML, not the invoice's conflicting $23,235 items 1–4 subtotal. The packing list has 1,176 hoodies against 1,200 invoiced and includes 24 Bangladesh-origin samples; the invoice adds those samples separately at $24 and excludes them from its subtotal. The invoice also discloses $4,180 buyer-supplied blouse fabric, excluded from the stated total. Its dutiability and allocation need broker confirmation. Freight ($3,850) and insurance ($185) are shown separately under CIF terms and are not added to the draft line values.

The resulting XML is an editable, non-transmitting draft, not a filing-ready entry. The documents do not provide the importer tax ID, broker's processing port, bond/payment setup, validated manufacturer ID, or full 10-digit HTSUS codes. The invoice totals and line quantities also conflict. I surfaced each gap rather than inventing identifiers or a duty result.

With more time, I would add scanned-PDF OCR, structured extraction with field-level source citations and confidence, a correction loop, broker profile integration, validated current HTSUS and trade-remedy lookups, assist/value allocation, and automated XSD validation in the upload workflow. I would evaluate extraction against a varied labeled set of invoices, packing lists, and transport documents before treating any output as file-ready.
